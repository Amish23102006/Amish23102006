B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) student at **VIT Bhopal** (2024–2028). I interned in **AI & Data Analytics at Tech Mahindra** and build projects in machine learning, NLP, computer vision, and AI agents.

- 🔬 Researching **cost-aware active learning**: cutting text-annotation cost with uncertainty and knapsack-based sample selection

- 📷 Exploring classical computer vision, from feature matching to panorama stitching
- 🤝 Open to internships and collaborations in AI/ML
